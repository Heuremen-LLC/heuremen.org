Residual excited population after spaced X-gate trains

Runs. ibm_fez, ibm_kingston, ibm_boston, ibm_pittsburgh, ibm_aachen, ibm_phoenix, 2–7 October 2026 (job IDs in the data file). A qubit receives 2, 6 or 10 X gates separated by 30 µs waits. Its excited population is compared with what its own measured decay predicts.

On this page, brackets are 95 % intervals: each processor’s is a 2000-draw bootstrap over qubits, and a combined bracket is the inverse-variance combination of the four processors’ values and widths.

What the data show

  • Slope per gate on the own-interval estimator: fez −0.00182, kingston −0.00198 (day 1) and −0.00136 (day 2), boston −0.00139 (day 1), −0.00154 / −0.00080 / −0.00063 (day 2 reads), pittsburgh −0.00107, phoenix −0.00002 and −0.00010 (day 2), −0.00058 (day 1). The share of individual qubits that are negative is 58–70 % on fez, kingston and boston (54–62 % on aachen, 46–57 % on phoenix, and pittsburgh 58 %, 87 of 150, sign test p = 0.06, not resolved, script, output).

Simulation

Simulated qubits with perfect gates and a shot-to-shot spread in decay rate, set from the measured non-exponential decay, give the same negative slope through the same estimator, so the data do not require a gate cost: the simulation.

The extra-gate test (22 October)

With the decay shape held equal (X X X against X on the same waits, 2, 6 and 10 steps, on fez, kingston, marrakesh and phoenix), an extra X gate costs 0.0001643 [0.00005906, 0.0002695] combined. Its difference from IBM’s stated X error is −0.0002279 [−0.0003419, −0.0001139], and the train does not see everything the stated error includes, so this is not a claim that the gates are better than stated. On fez and kingston, where both were measured, the per-gate residual above is opposite in sign to the extra-gate cost and at least twice its upper edge (0.00027), so it is not a gate cost there. No extra-gate test was run on boston or pittsburgh. Plan, code and output: deciding runs.

Run it

Download the script and the files in data/ into one folder (data in data/), then python3 verify_gate_cost.py. Standard library only. It prints each figure beside the recomputed value. The compact data were cut from the raw results. The raw files and the reduction scripts are available on request.
