IBM calibration records, September 2026

IBM’s own calibration records as we pulled them, behind the record notes of the Quantum Idle Audit QIA-2026-001 (the record notes). The October records the other rows use are saved beside each run, in that run’s folder.

  • Rows 3 and 4 (T1 and T2 dates, kingston and marrakesh, records stamped 26 to 28 September): ibm_kingston, ibm_marrakesh; ibm_fez records of the same days: ibm_fez. One gzipped JSON per day, as returned by IBM.
  • Row 5 (T1 and T2 under a new record stamp): the daily medians of six processors, 15 September to 6 October, flagged 8 day pairs (a ninth, phoenix 6 Oct 12:25, was pulled after) whose record stamp changed while the T1/T2 medians did not. The per-qubit check pulls both records of each pair from IBM’s properties history (no job) and compares every qubit: all 2,655 T1 and T2 entries (9 record pairs, including a third phoenix record of 6 October) keep the same value and the same measurement date under the new stamp (script, output).
  • Row 6 (zz on five live edges): zz_history.py reads every published record (output): fez 13–14 and 39–53 at 7.45 and 2.89 kHz on 26 to 29 September, phoenix 100–101, 70–80, 86–96 at 17.46, 14.13 and 13.68 kHz on 5 and 6 October, all 0.00 in the records of 21 and 22 October.
  • Record notes 2 to 4, 7 and 8: record_notes.py (output).
