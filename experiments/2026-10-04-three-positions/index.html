October campaign — runs, code, data

Jobs created 2–7 October 2026 (US Eastern), seven IBM processors: 305 jobs, 63 on 2 October, 43 on 4, 26 on 5, 48 on 6 and 125 on 7; three errored. The 32 equal-idle jobs of 7 October are among them (7 October). On 11 October ibm_fez ran 14 jobs: 3 of the measured qubit after 1 to 128 readouts (used in the paper) and 11 of a three-box probe-strength sweep. The 305 jobs measured untouched qubits left to precess while other qubits were read out, the measured qubits’ own decay, and each qubit’s decay during idle time. The findings that rest on these runs are on the supporting measurements pages, each with its own script and data.
