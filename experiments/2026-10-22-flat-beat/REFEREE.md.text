# Referee report: ibm_phoenix flat-beat run (zz_flat)  -- VERDICT: NARROW

Scripts (all in this folder): common.py, r1.py..r6.py, sim.py. Nothing submitted, no keys touched.

## 1. Recompute
Re-running the committed analysis from the raw npz reproduces Q1-Q4 exactly: slope 0.1931, rho 0.390, median 12.9 kHz, median ratio 0.463, 37 of 40 pairs.
All 37 pairs have >=10 live taus (floor 0.063); 35 have >=20. The P/Z ratio curves show clean |cos| nulls and full revivals (e.g. A=37/B=27, stated 49.6 kHz: ratio 0.99, 0.91, 0.81 at 2, 6, 10 us; null 0.08 at 30 us; back to 0.96 at 58 us), so the beat is real and sits near 15-17 kHz, not 50 kHz.

## 2. Fit artefact: NO (the instrument can see the calibrated beat)
Simulation: exact taus, each pair's stated zz, smoothed measured C_Z decay, 4000-shot binomial noise on both arms with random phase, B's phoenix T1 included via the exact "B in + decays" model (60 repeats, same fit, same floor):

Observed (0.19, 12.9, 0.39, 0.46) is reproduced by none. The instrument does not flatten a calibrated beat, and does not alias 10-58 kHz down to ~12 kHz. A constant factor (e.g. ZZ/2) would give slope 1, median ratio 0.5 (row "half-track") and is excluded as the explanation of the slope.
Model-free check (no fit): ratio P/Z at the tau of IBM's predicted first null 1/(2 zz): median 0.73 (should be ~0 if tracking; only 14 % of pairs < 0.3). Observed first-dip tau median 38 us vs predicted 17.6 us; f from first dip: median 13.2 kHz, log-log slope vs stated 0.16, Spearman 0.37 (p 0.03). Independent of the cosine fit and the 1-100 kHz grid.

## 3. Known-good grid
Fez known-good (fit works, slope 1.00, median fit/stated 0.96) is on a 3-303 us grid and stated zz median 6 kHz, i.e. it never tests 30-60 kHz. It validates the fit code, not the phoenix grid or the high-zz regime. The planted-track simulation above on the exact phoenix grid fills that gap (slope 1.00-1.02 at stated 11-59 kHz). Cite that, not fez, for the phoenix grid.

## 4. T1 / leakage / convention
B's relaxation only lowers the fit slightly (about -0.4 kHz at 12.5; track stays 1.02). Phoenix B T1 median 187 us (one outlier 17 us). Leakage/double-excitation are not modelled; nothing in the data (full-contrast revivals, minima ~0.05-0.1) points to them. IBM stores zz as general entries "zz_<ab>", unit GHz, one name scheme for all 218 entries, all >= 0, 8 exactly 0; nothing indicates a per-edge convention. Per-edge convention variation cannot be excluded from the record, but it would need to compress 11-59 kHz onto 4-23 kHz, which is not a convention factor. Tell the bill the reading uses |zz| x 1e9 Hz as beat frequency (f = xi), the same mapping that gave 1.00 on fez.
Binned fitted beat: stated 0-15 kHz: 11.7 (n=5); 15-25: 11.0 (7); 25-32: 13.7 (16); 32-60: 15.4 (9). Ratio is ~1 at the low end and ~0.3-0.5 at the high end, so "median 0.46" summarises a sub-proportional relation, not a constant factor.

## 5. Dependence (block bootstrap over 20 triples, 4000)
slope 0.19 [-0.0001, 0.37]; rho 0.39 [0.03, 0.67]; median 12.9 [11.7, 14.25]. The lower slope bound touches 0. Within-A fixed-effects slope 0.17 [-0.15, 0.57]; within-A concordance 10 of 17. Restricting to pairs with >=30 live taus: slope 0.095 (n=31); >=40: 0.097 (n=27). So "above 0" is not robust; "far below 1" is (upper bounds 0.37-0.57 in every variant).

## 6. The three zz = 0.00 pairs
Fitting them anyway gives 13.9, 15.2, 10.1 kHz (A = 101/100, 70/80, 96/86): same beat as everyone else on edges IBM states as zero. They cannot enter a log-log fit, the exclusion is on the x variable, not the outcome, and including them (as a flat reading) would push toward 0, so exclusion does not bias toward "flat"; it makes the stated slope slightly larger. Report them as an extra line. (IBM zz = 0 likely means uncalibrated; rzz7_17 gate_error = 1 shows such edges exist.)

# PART II: direct O1 phase shift (analyze_o1_shift.py), judged with the flat beat
Scripts: o1.py o3.py o4.py o5.py. Readings recomputed from the committed script: both result files reproduce exactly (unheralded differs only by the header line; heralded identical).

## (a) Recompute
Reproduced. Phoenix heralded: 19 of 21 triples, S2 0.486 [0.412, 0.599], S3 0.100 [0.006, 0.220], rho 0.486. Phoenix unheralded S3 -0.04 [-1.85, 1.97] is uninformative: two triples return 0.2 and 0.1 kHz (unwrap slips in the low-coherence tail, estimator is unweighted polyfit over all live taus), which the heralded run fixes (13.1 and 13.0). Do not quote the unheralded phoenix slope.
Third, alias-free estimator (mine, post hoc, supporting only): weighted periodogram of exp(i(phi_O1 - phi_Z)) over +-100 kHz (full Nyquist for 5 us steps), 25 Hz grid. Heralded phoenix: every one of 20 triples peaks at -9 to -18 kHz (peak coherence 0.93-1.00), median |f| 13.2, log-log slope vs stated 0.20, rho 0.75, including the edge stated 0.00 (15.3 kHz) and the 49.6 and 56.9 kHz edges (16.8, 14.8 kHz). Sign is negative on all 20. Unheralded periodogram: median 12.8, slope -0.06, 19 of 20 within 0.3 kHz of heralded.

## (b) Unwrapping aliasing: NO
A 57 kHz shift is 1.79 rad per 5 us step, below pi; aliasing needs > 100 kHz, and a 13 kHz reading would need a true 187 or 213 kHz, far above any stated zz (max 59 kHz). Simulation (actual triples, measured Z amplitudes, A detuning random +-30 kHz common to both arms, binomial noise, the committed unwrap-polyfit estimator with the committed floor), truth = stated zz: heralded median |df|/stated 0.998, slope 0.95; unheralded with B1 T1 decay 0.997 (slope noisy, 1.7, because of tail unwrap slips). Truth = flat 13 kHz: ratio 0.50, slope 0.00 (heralded). Observed 0.486 / 0.10 matches flat, not track. T1 decay of B1 does not explain the 0.5 ratio.

## (c) Heralding bias: negligible
Readout misassignment of 2 % (and 5 %) of B1 mixes in unshifted shots; it moves ratio by <0.001 and slope by <0.01 in simulation. Survivorship on B1=1 at the end selects shots where B1 stayed 1 for the whole time (decay-and-re-excite is second order), so it removes the T1 dilution rather than adding bias. The heralded fez ratio 1.075 on the same code shows the herald does not create a 0.5. Caveat from the file itself: heralded was defined after seeing unheralded; on fez it passes the ratio but its slope 0.644 [0.31, 0.87] misses 1 (outliers: edge stated 9.7 gives 17.6; stated 6.6 gives 0.5 and 0.7), so the known-good rule is only met for the unheralded variant, and unheralded is the one that fails on phoenix for estimator reasons. IBM will say exactly this. The answer is not the slope but the ratio-and-level: fitted shifts are within 1-2 kHz of one another across a 6x range of stated.

## (d) A detuning / drift: cancels
Arms are interleaved and shuffled in one job; copy 0 and copy 1 give the same per-triple shift (median absolute copy difference 0.24 kHz of 13). A's own detuning (phase slope in arm Z) is triple-specific, -26 to +22 kHz, uncorrelated with the 13 kHz shift; the simulation with random common detuning leaves the estimate unchanged. Extra mechanism test from the same job: with a Hahn echo on A (EP1 vs EZ), the beat goes away (fitted beat 1.3-3.7 kHz, one 7 kHz, versus 13 kHz in P1/Z), i.e. the effect is a static, refocusable shift as ZZ should be, not noise or B1 dynamics.

## Combined reading
Two different observables on different qubit states (B in |+>, envelope beat, 37 fresh pairs, pre-registered fit) and (B in |1> vs |0>, A's phase, 19 triples, post hoc, direct), two different estimators, two different jobs, fresh vs earlier As, agree: on ibm_phoenix the frequency shift of an untouched qubit when one neighbour changes state is about 13 kHz (range 9-17), nearly independent of the edge, for stated zz of 0 to 59 kHz. Same code on ibm_fez gives ratio 0.85 unheralded and 1.075 heralded and shifts that follow the stated values (rho 0.71-0.73). The flat-beat prediction's literal slope 0 is not met in either reading (0.1-0.2); the honest description is "compressed to a narrow band, weakly monotone".

## IBM's best rebuttals, and what answers them
1. "Stale or different-time calibration." Static ZZ does not move 4x across all edges; props timestamp 06:28 vs firing ~08:00; the edge stated 0.00 reads 15 kHz, and fez, same procedure, matches. Unanswered by the record: whether phoenix's zz_ entries come from a different protocol (e.g. coupler-on idle vs the circuit's actual idle). Ask IBM which.
2. "Factor-2 convention." Gives ratio 0.5 and slope 1 (simulated: half-track slope 1.05); observed slope 0.1-0.2 and fitted values constant. Fails.

It carries weight only because it agrees with the pre-registered reading and the alias-free periodogram agrees with both.
4. "Aliasing / unwrap." Excluded by simulation (b) and by the periodogram.
5. "A is detuned / drifting." Cancels (d).
6. "Low n." 19 + 37 pairs, but they share chip, day and calibration; this is one chip. Nothing here says anything about other chips; claim phoenix only.
