Three-box weak values, 22 October 2026

In the three-box weak measurement the box with the negative weak value reads about half of its textbook value (−0.53 on ibm_fez over a fourfold range of probe strength, 11 October; −0.50 to −0.69 on three processors, 24 September), while the positive boxes read about 0.8. This run asks whether that shortfall follows the negative sign or the |11⟩ corner, by moving the negative box (variants V11, V10, VA), and compares every box with a standard noise model (qiskit-aer) built from IBM’s calibration in force when the job fires. Plan, circuits, analysis, known-good check and synthetic test were pushed before any job.

Addendum, written after the jobs were submitted and before any result was read: the question has four answers (sign, corner, both, neither), not two; and an outside referee of the 11 October data found that a standard noise model plus one common probe offset reproduces those readings, so the offset is now a reading of its own. addendum · addendum_fourstate.py · planted test · referee report · hashes

Results (ibm_marrakesh; the fez job was cancelled while queued, why)

  • Probe offset. In the box whose ideal probe value is 0, the probe reads +0.049 to +0.081 (±0.010) in the three variants; the standard noise model built from IBM’s calibration at firing gives about +0.005, and IBM’s stated readout asymmetry of the probe qubit (q8) adds +0.006. The same offset appeared on ibm_fez on 11 October (+0.062) and in the 24 September runs (+0.060).
  • The negative box reads −0.59 to −0.60 against the model’s −0.76; the positive boxes also read 0.06 to 0.18 below the model. The shortfall relative to the model is shared by all boxes.
  • Sign or corner. The difference between the negative box and the positive boxes is consistent with zero in all three variants. The four-state reading names the sign on this one chip by a margin of 0.001; the addendum fixed before the data says a single-chip reading of that kind is not a finding. Not decided.
