Direct per-edge zz, 22 and 23 October 2026

Does IBM’s stated zz predict the frequency shift an untouched qubit feels when one neighbour goes from 0 to 1? On ibm_phoenix a run with fit and analysis fixed before the job found the beat beside a superposed neighbour does not scale with the stated values (log-log slope 0.19; ibm_fez 1.00). This run removes the beat fit: the shift between neighbour-in-0 and neighbour-in-1 is the zz itself. Same phoenix pairs, ibm_fez as the control. Plan, circuits, analysis and synthetic test were pushed before any job.

plan · direct.py · analyze_direct.py · synth_direct.py · synthetic results · hashes: plan, code

The fez job dbd5gl7i0auc7394o30g (job record), submitted 22 October 18:39 UTC, was read the same night by the committed analysis, unchanged (output; fetch_direct.py saves the raw bits and IBM’s calibration in force at submission, pulled from IBM’s properties history afterwards because it was not saved at firing).

  • Known-good met on ibm_fez (66 edges, all fitted): the shift tracks IBM’s stated zz, log-log slope 0.923 [0.832, 1.018] (contains 0.99), median shift over stated 1.037 [1.016, 1.078] (inside 0.4 to 2.5); rank correlation 0.885 [0.755, 0.969]. The method reads IBM’s zz directly where IBM’s figure is right.
  • Edges IBM states as zz = 0.00 on ibm_fez: 13–14 shifts by 8.3 kHz and 39–53 by 2.8 kHz (106–107, stated 0.8 kHz, shifts 1.6). Copies agree (8.33 and 8.25; 2.64 and 3.05; script, output), and IBM’s own records of 26 to 29 September gave these two edges 7.45 and 2.89 kHz (record history).
  • ibm_phoenix, job dbd9789gv2cc738hl070 (job record), submitted 22 October 22:52 UTC, ran 23 October 05:33 UTC, read by the committed analysis, unchanged (output; IBM’s record saved at firing, props, and the next record, props, carry the same 218 zz values). Same 20 triples as the flat-beat run; 37 edges with stated zz above 1 kHz (11.6 to 58.8 kHz, median 28.3). The shift does not track the stated zz: log-log slope −0.061 [−0.629, 0.315] (below 1), median shift over stated 0.486 [0.452, 0.528] (below 1), rank correlation 0.245 [−0.170, 0.630]. Copies agree. A single convention factor would leave the slope near 1 (the plan’s synthetic factor-2 world reads 0.996).
  • Fit quality, defined after the data (outside referee): the two edges of phoenix qubit 41 have phase-fit residuals of 1.64 and 1.27 rad, a slipped unwrap (median 0.077). The same rule, residual above 0.5 rad, removes two edges of fez qubit 103 (1.34 and 1.19 rad). Without them: phoenix slope 0.192 [0.041, 0.370], median ratio 0.484 [0.452, 0.520], rank correlation 0.418 [0.082, 0.683]; fez slope 0.942 [0.864, 1.030], median ratio 1.034 (script, output). The slope stays below 1 either way and matches the flat-beat run’s 0.19 [0.01, 0.38]; the rank correlation depends on the two edges.
  • Edges IBM states as zz = 0.00 on ibm_phoenix: 100–101 shifts by 14.2 kHz, 70–80 by 15.4, 86–96 by 10.2; copies agree (14.03 and 14.40; 15.09 and 15.63; 10.09 and 10.39; script, output). The beat fit applied to them after the flat-beat data gave 13.9, 15.2 and 10.1.
  • What this run cannot separate: the neighbour’s state from the gate that sets it (the phase grows linearly from zero, as a state-dependent shift does); next-nearest neighbours were not controlled. One job per chip, one day.
