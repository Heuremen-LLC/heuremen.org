Temporal and spatial correlations, and a three-box weak measurement — code and data

24 September 2026, IBM ibm_marrakesh, replicated on ibm_kingston and ibm_fez. These runs have no equal-idle arm on untouched qubits and are not used for the readout claims of the paper. Two-qubit (spatial) and one-qubit sequential (temporal) correlations against a rotation angle, and a three-box weak measurement (third-box weak value −0.506 ± 0.057). The temporal and spatial curves follow the same cos θ, as standard quantum mechanics predicts.

On this page, ± is one standard error of the weak value, computed from the spread of the probe readings over the shots kept after post-selection.

Everything behind the page. Python 3.12 with qiskit, qiskit-ibm-runtime, qiskit-aer. Put your own IBM Quantum API key in a file and point the scripts at it. Every script has a free sim mode: rehearse there first, then write your prediction down, then spend real minutes.
