Delivered Hahn-echo T2 against IBM’s stated T2, 22 and 23 October 2026

IBM states T2 from a Hahn echo (“T2 is reported from a Hahn echo sequence”, IBM QPU information guide). These runs measure the Hahn-echo T2 of the same qubits and compare it with the stated value qubit by qubit: P(1) after SX, τ/2, X, τ/2, SX† at τ = 0 to 400 µs, two sparse sets per chip (no two measured qubits coupled), each circuit twice, 2000 shots. Each plan was pushed before its jobs. The reading is the median over a chip’s qubits of delivered over stated T2, with a 4000-draw bootstrap over qubits, under the planned fit (P(1) = 1/2 + A e−τ/T2) and under a fit with a free asymptote (the model of IBM’s open-source T2HahnAnalysis), which was added after runs 1 and 2 were read (addendum).

On this page, brackets are 95 % intervals (2.5th to 97.5th percentile) from a 4000-draw bootstrap over qubits.

Every run

Every run is listed below, on ibm_kingston, ibm_marrakesh, ibm_fez, ibm_pittsburgh, ibm_phoenix and ibm_boston. The known-good (median P(1) at τ = 0 at least 0.90) is met on every run. “Ran” is when IBM started the job (UTC, from the job record), and the age is the median age of the stated T2 values at that moment. IBM’s record was saved when each job was submitted, and boston, phoenix and pittsburgh waited in IBM’s queue for 14.6, 6.4 and 1.8 hours. The fit model moves a chip’s reading by up to 0.120 (boston).

Readings

  • Runs of one chip pooled by qubit (runs 1 and 2, same IBM record): kingston 0.942 [0.885, 0.987] BELOW (fixed fit), 0.941 [0.898, 0.975] BELOW (free fit), marrakesh 0.925 [0.889, 1.012] CONSISTENT (fixed fit), 0.887 [0.835, 0.925] BELOW (free fit), fez 1.034 [0.977, 1.067] CONSISTENT (fixed fit), 0.981 [0.941, 1.034] CONSISTENT (free fit) (kingston and marrakesh, fez). Kingston’s three runs (1, 2 and fresh record) pooled by qubit: 0.953 [0.910, 0.993] BELOW (fixed fit), 0.933 [0.902, 0.956] BELOW (free fit) (output). Marrakesh’s three runs (1, 2 and fresh record) pooled by qubit: 0.972 [0.929, 1.022] CONSISTENT (fixed fit), 0.889 [0.845, 0.936] BELOW (free fit) (output). An earlier combination of runs 1 and 2 by inverse variance treated the same qubits as independent and is withdrawn (addendum).
  • Between jobs under one unchanged record the planned-fit reading moves: kingston 0.910 to 0.946 and marrakesh 0.907 to 0.948 between jobs submitted 7 minutes apart (the free-asymptote fit: 0.916 to 0.949 and 0.892 to 0.897). Under the planned fit both shifts are more than 3 standard deviations beyond shot noise (two outside referees: about 4.5 and 3.5 on 22 October, and 3.4 and 3.3 on 23 October by a binomial redraw of both runs, output). A single run is one draw of a quantity that moves between jobs. The pool addendum says “about 20 min apart” and the clock-floor addendum “twenty minutes apart”, while the job records show 7 minutes.
  • Clock on fez (plan, floor addendum): IBM’s record was restamped twice between the fez points while all 155 stated T2 values and their dates stayed the same, so the stated T2 is fixed. Other entries did change (single-qubit gate errors on 148 qubits before clock 1, CZ errors on 340 pairs before clock 2, record comparison). The clock plan stops the clock if IBM’s record changes. It was read as continuing because the T2 values did not change. At 0.7, 1.0, 1.8 and 3.8 hours after IBM’s T2 measurement: fixed fit 1.051 → 1.014 → 1.000 → 1.019, decline +0.032 against a floor of 0.082, and free fit 0.979 → 1.006 → 0.977 → 1.005, decline -0.026 against a floor of 0.066. CONSISTENT under both fits: no decline larger than 0.082 (planned fit) or 0.066 (free fit) over the first 3.8 hours. The planned points at 8 and 16 hours were not run.
  • Record age. Within a chip most stated T2 values share one measurement time (kingston 153 of 154, marrakesh 143 of 153, fez 152 of 155). The age test (defined after the data) has unbounded intervals on kingston and fez, and on marrakesh its older group is 2 qubits, so it cannot tell. Across chips, age and chip go together and are not separated.
  • Reporting rule (addendum, written before the fresh and wider runs were read): a chip is reported as below its stated T2 only if a run on a record whose stated T2 values are under 3 hours old reads BELOW under both fits, and all its runs pooled by qubit read BELOW under both fits. No chip meets it. Kingston: IBM remeasured its T2 in the record of 23 October 06:25 EDT, and the fresh-record run (plan, addendum), on stated values 0.6 hours old, reads BELOW under the planned fit and CONSISTENT under the free fit, so kingston does not meet the rule, although its three runs pooled read BELOW under both. Marrakesh: IBM remeasured its T2 in the record of 23 October 15:17 EDT, and the fresh-record run, on stated values 0.5 hours old, reads CONSISTENT under both fits (1.015 [0.937, 1.130] planned, 0.958 [0.902, 1.016] free), so marrakesh does not meet the rule. Its three runs pooled read CONSISTENT under the planned fit and BELOW under the free fit. Phoenix’s two fits disagree, on stated values 27.7 hours old. ibm_boston (job, plan, output) reads CONSISTENT under both fits, on stated values 34.3 hours old.

Per qubit, across runs (defined after the data): whether the same qubits read below their stated T2 in every run (persistence.py → output), and how qubits ranked high and low by their stated T2 deliver against it, with a simulation in which every qubit delivers exactly its stated T2 (trap.py → output).

What these runs cannot do

IBM’s own fit window and model for the stated value are not published. Qubits whose decay is not a single exponential are read for their shape as well as their time, and the two fits differ by up to 0.120. Qubits with a stated T2 well above 400 µs are fitted on the early decay only. The free-asymptote fit in hahn_pool.py is unbounded, though its description says bounded to [0, 1]. A bounded refit by the outside referee agrees within 0.002 on every run. Each chip was read on one processor-day, and the chips were read on records of different ages.
