Does the coherence that readouts cost untouched qubits come from a neighbour hidden in a level that reads as 0? Every measured qubit gets an X after the last step: a real 0 moves to the 1 cluster, a leaked state does not, and the loss is re-read keeping only shots in which every wired neighbour flipped.
