# Check K: the kick per readout in two segments, offset-free (the parts of R2.2)
Defined 22 Oct 2026 after the count run's output was seen, before this was computed. Heuremen LLC. Script check_k_segments.py.
R2.2 is late minus early; this prints its two parts. Per spectator, the unwrapped phase gap tot(N) (as analyze.py count): early slope
(tot(9) - tot(1)) / 8, late slope (tot(81) - tot(27)) / 54, in degrees per readout. A fixed per-circuit offset cancels in both.
Read with analyze.py's reading() (copy gap, pooled, four outcomes) and summary() (chips by value and width).
