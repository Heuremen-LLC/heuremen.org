Dynamical decoupling run, 22 October 2026

Does a symmetric echo (X, X) on the untouched qubits during each step remove the readout phase kick and the readout coherence cost? The echo is applied identically in the idle arm and the readout arm, on ibm_fez, ibm_kingston and ibm_marrakesh, with the same measured qubits as the control run. Plan, circuits and analysis pushed before any job. Results, read by the committed analysis, are below.

Results, read by the committed analysis (full output), after review by an outside referee

Jobs: fez dbcus97i0auc7394ddkg, kingston dbcuqa0c77vs738739g0, marrakesh dbcur9ni0auc7394dbmg (fez, kingston, marrakesh fire logs, each verifying that every untouched qubit carries exactly two X per step with the echo and none without, and that every step lasts exactly the readout length in both). The built-in null (identical circuits labelled echo on and off at zero readouts) reads consistent with 0 on all three. Each processor measures 39 qubits, the same as in the control run.

  • The echo removes most of the kick. Per readout, −1.50, −3.24, −2.50° without the echo and −0.09, +0.45, −0.27° with it (fez, kingston, marrakesh). The change is resolved on all three, and the small residuals disagree across processors and are not combined. Splitting the kick by the echo’s sign pattern puts 45 to 57 % of it in the middle half of the readout window. These runs do not separate a shift during the readout from a control-system phase-frame update.
  • Whether the echo changes the coherence cost cannot be told from this run. No change is resolved at 2, 4 or 8 readouts on fez and marrakesh, or at 2 and 4 on kingston (kingston at 8: copies disagree), but the interval ends reach 0.5 to 1.3 times the size of the cost itself, so an unchanged, removed or doubled cost all fit. At 16 readouts fez reads a larger cost with the echo (+0.008). The echo also changes the idle arm’s own coherence, by amounts that differ in sign between processors.
  • Prior art. A randomized-benchmarking suite detecting errors on measured and nearby unmeasured (spectator) qubits on IBM hardware is in Govia et al. (arXiv:2207.04836). A randomized-benchmarking protocol that measures the rate at which mid-circuit measurements induce crosstalk errors in many-qubit circuits, on a trapped-ion and a 27-qubit IBM processor, including how much dynamical decoupling removes, is in Hothem et al. (arXiv:2410.16706). Govia et al.’s tomography of one measured and one unmeasured qubit on ibm_peekskill found the largest error to have a structure indicative of a coherent Z-phase on the unmeasured qubit. In a surface-code memory on ibm_phoenix, an XX echo on data qubits during ancilla readout lowers the per-round logical error rate of an X-basis memory by 21 and 38 % on two placements, in a circuit that also uses short readout and fused measurement and reset (Kan et al., arXiv:2610.23840). What this run adds: the same echo in both arms, phase and coherence read separately, per-processor numbers.
