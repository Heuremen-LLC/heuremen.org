# Portfolio Run, wording correction (23 Oct 2026, /q check 4, after the verdict; numbers unchanged)
'Predicted from IBM's figures' means the estimated success probability (ESP): the product of (1 - IBM's published error) over every
gate and readout in the compiled circuit -- the score Qiskit's own layout passes use. IBM publishes no prediction. Headline B therefore
reads: delivered success was 11 to 29 % below the success the product of IBM's published error figures gives, on 12 of 12 placements.
The formula charges each readout IBM's average readout_error; an all-zeros answer pays only the smaller 0->1 error, so the ESP is lower
than IBM's own figures strictly allow, which makes the reported shortfall conservative.
