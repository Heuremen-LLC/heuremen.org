Readout vs equal idle

Does a mid-circuit readout advance an untouched neighbouring qubit’s phase more than an idle of exactly the same length? IBM processors, 21 October 2026.

On this page, every bracket [lo, hi] is a 95 % interval from a 2000-draw bootstrap over untouched qubits, including the two quoted from the excitation test.

Design

Per chip (156 qubits): 39 measured qubits, 117 untouched qubits prepared on the equator. Each step is one of three modes. D: measured qubits idle for exactly the published readout length. M0: measured qubits read out, held in 0. M1: prepared in 1, then read out. Barriers on all qubits after every step so untouched qubits see identical scheduling in every mode. We run 0, 2, 4, 8 and 16 steps in the X and Y quadratures, with 2000 shots and one job per chip. Ratio R0 = phase per step in M0 ÷ phase per step in D. Untouched qubits kept if their D slope is at least 1°/step (declared in advance, and results without the cut are printed too).

The plan and analysis were written and hashed before any job was submitted (plan file 14:52:48, first job submitted 51 s later, local time): PREREG.md (served as .text so the bytes match the hash), SHA-256 0a8bc2b627a737f8… (full hash). Fez, Marrakesh and Kingston were the declared chips. Pittsburgh was added afterwards with the same hashed design and analysis. Boston was submitted and errored.

What it says (analysis added after outside review)

The ratio above divides a phase excess by each untouched qubit’s own idle phase, so its value depends on which slowly precessing untouched qubits are kept. The excess itself is a phase per readout:

chipextra phase per readout, measured qubits in 0 (median, 95 %)on untouched qubits precessing backwards
ibm_fez+1.73° [1.29, 2.21]+1.49°
ibm_kingston+2.41° [1.96, 3.58]+3.28°
ibm_marrakesh+2.13° [1.44, 2.79]+3.01°
ibm_pittsburgh+1.79° [1.16, 2.66]+2.75°

Extra elapsed time would make the excess negative for untouched qubits precessing backwards. The excess is positive. The M1 arm here is compared with an idle whose measured qubits are in 0, so M1 − D includes a phase that appears without any readout when the measured qubits are in 1. The excitation test has the matching idle-in-1 arm and shows the kick is not larger with the measured qubits in 1 (kick in 1 minus kick in 0: +0.204 [−0.092, +0.320] on kingston, larger in 1 by at most 0.32°, consistent with zero, and −0.673 [−0.982, −0.403] on marrakesh, smaller in 1). A regression of the excess on the idle phase (in kick_analysis.py) is biased by noise in the idle phase and is not interpreted.
