Mid-circuit readout and the qubits it does not touch: technical summary

This page restates results through 22 October 2026. Later runs, such as the Hahn T2 line, are on the front page and their experiment pages.

IBM processors (ibm_fez, ibm_kingston, ibm_marrakesh, ibm_pittsburgh, ibm_boston, ibm_aachen; 156 qubits each), ibm_phoenix (120 qubits, recorded as a square lattice) and Rigetti Cepheus-1-108Q; September–October 2026. Figures link to their data; the supporting figures are recomputed by standard-library scripts from compact per-qubit data, whose reduction from raw I/Q is available on request.

1. A phase kick per readout (21 October)

39 qubits per chip are read out N times (N = 0–16); 117 untouched qubits run a Ramsey sequence; each readout step is paired with an idle of exactly the published measure duration (the default measure instruction) in the same job. Median extra phase per readout over untouched qubits, measured qubits in 0 (sizes; every kick is in the same direction): +1.73° [1.29, 2.21] fez, +2.41° [1.96, 3.58] kingston, +2.13° [1.44, 2.79] marrakesh, +1.79° [1.16, 2.66] pittsburgh. With an idle arm that also holds the measured qubits in 1, the kick in 1 is 0.18° (fez) and 0.92° (marrakesh) per readout smaller than in 0 (22 October). On kingston the first 22 October job’s kick in |0⟩ had shifted copies (cannot tell) and the difference reads +0.066 [−0.005, +0.229], consistent with zero; the repeat job bounds any increase to about 0.1° (+0.049 [−0.098, +0.132], copies agree). In the 21 October excitation test, re-read after the data, the difference (kick in 1 minus kick in 0) is +0.204 [−0.092, +0.320] on kingston (larger in 1 by at most 0.32°; consistent with zero) and −0.673 [−0.982, −0.403] on marrakesh (smaller in 1); the runs are reported beside each other and not combined. Holding the measured qubits in 1 without any readout shifts the untouched qubits’ phase by 3.10° (kingston) and 3.12° (marrakesh) per step on 21 October, and 2.5 to 3.4° on fez, kingston and marrakesh on 22 October. output · difference. Qubits precessing backwards gain it too, so extra elapsed time cannot be all of it; a smaller stretch on top is not excluded. A per-qubit regression of the excess on the idle phase is biased by noise in the idle phase (errors in variables) and is not used for physics. Cause not identified. data · idle-in-1 arm

On ibm_marrakesh the kick depends on which readout instruction is used (shorter instruction: 0.58 [0.43, 0.73] and 0.51 [0.36, 0.65] of the longer one’s kick at 4 and 8 readouts); on ibm_kingston, in repeats, the ratio is 0.95 [0.75, 1.17] and 0.98 [0.77, 1.28] at 4 and 8 readouts (2000 shots), consistent with the full kick and excluding the length ratio 0.63; fez cannot tell, also at 4000 shots. readout-instruction run

1b. Echo, wear-out and the 7 October runs

A symmetric echo (X, X) on the untouched qubits in every step of both arms removes most of the kick: per readout −1.50, −3.24, −2.50° without the echo and −0.09, +0.45, −0.27° with it (fez, kingston, marrakesh); the residual is resolved on all three, including +0.45 on kingston, opposite in sign, and is not combined. These runs do not separate a shift during the readout from a control-system phase-frame update. Whether the echo changes the coherence cost cannot be told. Against 72 equal idles, the kick after 72 readouts differs by +0.028 [−0.038, +0.085] (fez; its 72-idle arm had shifted copies), +0.006 [−0.034, +0.059] (kingston) and −0.036 [−0.118, +0.104] (marrakesh) degrees per readout, consistent with zero on all three; the kick after 8 readouts minus after 2 is consistent with 0 (+0.036, −0.008, +0.294°; at most 14, 6 and 24% of the kick). Past about 27 readouts the kick’s fade cannot be read with these qubits. The 7 October runs (1 to 25 measured qubits, 32 jobs, recomputed with the same estimators and not pre-registered) read the kick below zero (the direction of the 21 and 22 October kick) over all untouched qubits in 13 of the 14 arms with 25 measured qubits on kingston (0.26 to 0.58° per readout) and within two couplings in 12 of 14 (0.69 to 1.38°); the arms share untouched qubits, no arm is below zero at five or more couplings, and the one arm above zero there is not distinguished from the false-reading floor. They are read beside the later runs and never pooled. echo run · wear-out · 7 October

2. Coherence and concurrence of untouched qubits (21 October)

48 Bell pairs and 21 single qubits among the untouched qubits; N = 0, 8, 16, 32 readouts or equal idles; concurrence (X-state form, five settings) and coherence |⟨X⟩ + i⟨Y⟩|, both insensitive to a phase kick. Coherence after readouts minus after the equal idle, median per qubit: −0.011 fez, −0.005 kingston, −0.013 marrakesh at N = 8 (intervals below 0), −0.010, −0.006, −0.014 at N = 32 (intervals reaching 0). Whether the cost compounds past 8 readouts cannot be read with these qubits (at 32 readouts the intervals reach 0). The planned primary reading (loss ratio at N = 32; plan hashed locally) read consistent with 1: a ratio dilutes a fixed deficit once the idle loss is large, so that ruler could not see it. Its concurrence half saturated (0 in both arms): cannot tell. A follow-up run recorded every mid-circuit readout: on marrakesh the coherence excess next to a measured qubit (+0.025) is far above what random excitation of the measured qubits (4.8 %) would produce given the measured phase a neighbour in 1 imposes (+0.0008), and +0.010 [0.000, 0.019] with no measured neighbour (31 qubits), where that model predicts zero; that model was applied after the data, because the planned prediction was zero by construction and tested nothing. On kingston the excess is +0.004 [−0.003, 0.014] against +0.0005, an interval that contains both 0 and the raw-readout run’s kingston cost. data · excitation test · saturated run

2b. Pre-registered control run (21 October, later)

Plan, circuits and analysis committed before the jobs (GitHub record: plan 23:00:47 UTC, first IBM job created 23:01:05, analysis 23:02:19, first job finished 23:02:31); every circuit twice per job, shuffled; ibm_fez, ibm_kingston, ibm_marrakesh. Kick per readout 1.6, 2.9, 2.4° (both copies). Number read out: 1 of 39 leaves at most 0.15° (fez, and marrakesh by a copy-gap check defined after the data, where the committed rule reads its copies as disagreeing) and 0.09° (kingston), and a fixed offset of the full kick is excluded on all three (every copy at least 1.4, 2.7 and 2.2° from it); 10 of 39 gives 0.13, 0.28, 0.23° (8 to 10% of the full kick, less than the proportional 26%; fez copies disagree by the committed rule, with no shift by copy gap; a 22 October repeat with the same code reads fez 0.17 [0.07, 0.32]° with both copies agreeing, 11% of that job’s full kick of 1.47°), so the kick is not proportional to the number read out; the subsets are one random draw (seed 20261021) and the single qubit is one position. Extra readout time: 95% upper bounds of 4, 1 and 12% on how much longer than stated the readout lasts (rounded up from 3.7, 0.8, 11.4%; bound defined after the data, from a slope fit dominated by the qubits that turn the other way). Coherence after readouts minus equal idle (117 unpaired untouched qubits): kingston −0.009 / −0.007 (N = 2 / 4; at 8 the copies disagree), marrakesh −0.011 / −0.016 (N = 2 / 8; at 4 the copies disagree), fez consistent with zero at 2 and 4 (at 8 the copies disagree). Concurrence per Bell pair, readout minus idle with all shots (negative is a loss): kingston −0.011 / −0.010 (N = 2 / 4), marrakesh −0.009 / −0.016, fez −0.005 at 4 (consistent with zero at 2); section 2c gives the reading with every measured neighbour verified. With every measured neighbour read 0 on every readout (this run’s selection; idle minus readout), the marrakesh cost is consistent with zero at 2 and 4 readouts (+0.0060 [−0.0020, +0.0115], +0.0056 [−0.0017, +0.0181]), while fez reads +0.004 at 4 and kingston +0.010 and +0.009 at 2 and 4, above zero. The pre-registered leakage reading was invalid as designed (readout misassignment of |0⟩ dominates it). plan, analysis, raw data

2c. Coherence and concurrence with every measured neighbour verified

Raw-readout run (22 October, 01:09 UTC): keeping only shots in which every measured neighbour was verified in its 0 cluster on every readout (97 to 99% of shots), the median untouched qubit next to a measured one keeps less coherence than after the same idle: +0.0056 and +0.0062 on kingston and +0.0087 and +0.0205 on marrakesh after 4 and 8 readouts, all above zero when the copies are pooled (kingston at 4: neither copy alone resolves it; at 2 readouts the committed rule reads both as cannot tell, the copies disagreeing, and a copy-gap reading defined after the data finds no shift, pooled +0.0054 and +0.0074, above zero). On fez the committed rule reads 2 and 4 readouts as cannot tell; the same copy-gap reading finds no shift between the copies, and the pooled reading, above zero, is +0.0042 at 2 readouts; at 4 it is +0.0057, but +0.0037 [−0.0001, +0.0100], consistent with zero, once readout assignment error is corrected (outside referee, defined after the data; readout-mitigation check); at 8 readouts it is consistent with zero. In the control run the cost after 8 readouts minus after 2 is +0.0043 on fez and +0.0120 on marrakesh (above zero) and +0.0047 on kingston (not resolved); the 31 untouched qubits per processor with no measured neighbour show no resolved coherence loss. Bell pairs keep 0.005 to 0.009 less concurrence after 4 readouts in the same selection (pairs whose idle concurrence is above 0; the selection can bias the loss upward, and the 39-minus-1 difference, which applies it to both arms, is the unbiased reading): resolved on fez (+0.0051 [+0.0005, +0.0126]; +0.0070 in the first run) and marrakesh (+0.0094 [+0.0037, +0.0243]; +0.0093), in two independent runs each, read side by side and never pooled; on kingston the committed rule reads the clean-shot reading as cannot tell in either run (copies disagree); a copy-gap check defined after the data finds no shift, and the pooled readings are above zero, +0.0100 [+0.0040, +0.0138] and +0.0068 [+0.0010, +0.0170]; the all-shot reading is above zero in both. The X-flip check: run 1 is void (its known-good condition was not met on any processor); in run 2 a neighbour hidden in a level that reads as 0 is excluded as the cause on marrakesh, to the extent the flip catches it; on fez it cannot tell, and on kingston its known-good condition was not met. Neighbours stuck in 1 (0.4 to 1.1% of shots) are not excluded as the whole cost. raw-readout run · pair run · pair repeat · flip check 1 · flip check 2

3. The measured qubit

Per readout, the measured qubit’s contrast loss equals 1.3–1.7× the stated readout duration of its own T1-like decay (per-chip means of three reads, seven processors, 4 to 6 October; individual reads 1.2–1.8; the conversion assumes single-exponential decay, which these qubits do not follow, so it is approximate). At equal elapsed time, with no assumption about the decay curve, the comparison is from ibm_fez (11 October) and the 22 October readout-instruction run (fez and marrakesh, and kingston in a repeat). On fez, 24 readouts leave 0.48–0.52 where idling leaves 0.62–0.66. ibm_fez, 11 October, three jobs (of 14 that day; the other 11 are a three-box probe-strength sweep), N = 1–128 readouts of a qubit prepared in 1: population falls as a stretched exponential with exponent 0.823 ± 0.012 (spread of three jobs), fits χ²/dof ≤ 1.38, crossing 1/2 at 23–26 readouts. loss per readout · decay data

4. Untouched-qubit time against the stated duration (October)

Untouched-qubit elapsed time over stated duration, seven processors, untouched qubits with |rate| > 4 deg/µs: 1.0207 ± 0.0092, 2.2σ from 1: cannot tell. This ratio mixes the readout kick (section 1) with each untouched qubit’s own spin rate, so it does not measure a duration in either direction. Slowly precessing untouched qubits bias long (Spearman −0.17 to −0.56). This fitted-rate reading is superseded by the equal-idle design. verify.py

5. Reach

With the 39 measured qubits packed in one region, untouched qubits 3 to 4 couplings from the nearest one are kicked by 0.81, 1.77 and 1.30° per readout (fez, kingston, marrakesh; 15 qubits, every interval above 0); at five or more couplings (92 qubits) the kick is consistent with zero on all three (+0.0039 [−0.029, +0.037] combined; that pool is dominated by qubits six or more couplings away, and at exactly 5 the reach run reads +0.055 [+0.002, +0.108] combined, at the false-reading floor). The reach run (three packed layouts) resolves the kick at 4 couplings with the three processors combined (+0.21 [+0.09, +0.32]°, about a tenth of the kick next to a measured qubit; fez +0.19, kingston +0.13, marrakesh +0.40) and at 3 couplings on fez and marrakesh singly; kingston at 3 couplings reads +0.51 [−0.005, +1.30] and the three combined +0.21 [−0.04, +0.45], both consistent with zero. 5 couplings is resolved combined (+0.055 [+0.002, +0.108]) and by no processor alone; 6 couplings and beyond are consistent with zero combined (upper ends 0.03 to 0.06°), while marrakesh alone reads 6 couplings above zero (+0.050 [+0.002, +0.155]). The committed edge rule names no combined edge; by processor the unbroken edge is 4 couplings on fez and marrakesh and none on kingston. Per-layout medians at the same distance differ several-fold. Readings were not corrected for multiplicity: synthetic runs with no kick resolve 6.0% of single-processor readings and 5.6% of combined ones, so the resolutions at 3 couplings on fez, at 5 couplings combined and at 6 couplings on marrakesh, whose lower ends are 0.001 to 0.002, are not distinguished from that floor. An earlier exploratory reading of 4 to 6 October data (rank correlation of untouched-qubit excess with Σ1/r² +0.16, 1283 readings, five processors) was weak and not reproducible across layouts. data · reach run

6. Gates

Spaced X-gate trains (2 October, 30 µs intervals): started in 0 with ten gates 30 µs apart, the level ends above one-exponential bookkeeping (+0.0200 ± 0.0016 on pittsburgh, +0.0274 ± 0.0028 on marrakesh, +0.0287 ± 0.0028 on kingston). Against each job’s own decay curve the excess is within ±0.007 on kingston and pittsburgh at intervals of 30 µs and longer, and on marrakesh it reverses sign by −0.012 to −0.018 (start 0) and up to −0.016 (start 1): the bookkeeping removes most of the excess at 30 µs and longer and over-corrects on marrakesh. At 5 and 10 µs a residual remains, and these runs cannot tell whether it is physical. The negative residual excited population per gate after spaced trains (−0.0006 to −0.0020 per gate, four processors) is reproduced by simulated qubits with perfect gates and a persistent shot-to-shot spread in decay rate matched to the measured non-exponential share (−0.0018 vs −0.0014 mean real). The spread is modelled, not measured. With the decay shape held equal (X X X against X on the same waits, 22 October), an extra X gate costs 0.0001643 [0.00005906, 0.0002695] combined over fez, kingston, marrakesh and phoenix, and its difference from IBM’s stated X error is −0.0002279 [−0.0003419, −0.0001139]; the train does not see everything the stated error includes, so this is not a claim that the gates are better than stated. On fez and kingston, where both were measured, the residual is opposite in sign to the extra-gate cost and at least twice its upper edge (0.00027), so it is not a gate cost there; no extra-gate test exists on boston or pittsburgh. simulation · data

7. Controls

Decay is not single-exponential: 62–85 % of qubit-copies sit below their own 300 µs extrapolation at 30 µs (six processors), where a pure exponential with the same noise gives 49–54 % (26 jobs, 4 to 7 October); the test cannot separate a non-exponential decay from a per-qubit systematic common to the three copies. A qubit left in |0⟩ for 300 µs sits 0.04 to 0.80 % above its own job’s zero-wait |0⟩ level in all 26 jobs on six processors (five of them under two widths); at 600 µs three jobs are at or below zero, so the offset does not grow but is not constant. Repeated measurements of a qubit’s decay time differ by up to 9.8 µs between jobs and 11.5 µs between copies inside one job; compared like with like, the range of three copies in a job is inside the simulated 95th percentile of shot noise alone in 8 of 9 jobs (marrakesh job 301: 10.4 against 8.9 µs); the simulation takes its noise scale from the copies themselves, so it cannot separate shot noise from drift between copies. data

8. Second vendor and textbook checks

A neighbour in |2⟩ shifts a coupled qubit’s precession rate by −54.93 ± 1.28 and −38.64 ± 1.62 °/µs on 28 September and 4 October (0.5–2 µs fit windows; shot noise only; the dates differ by 16.3 ± 2.1 °/µs). The pair is the same on both dates; between them the pulse amplitude was retuned, the number of simultaneous qubits changed from 1 to 106 and the processor was recalibrated, so the difference is not assigned to the coupling. Over 22 other pairs the shift spans −72.8 to +30.9 °/µs: on this processor sign and size are properties of the pair. From 24 September data, not used in the paper: temporal and spatial correlators follow cos θ; the three-box weak measurement gives a negative value for the negative box (see the three-box page); the earlier figures were −0.506 ± 0.057 for the weak value and +0.0012 ± 0.0024 for the extra decay of entanglement alone. neighbour data · textbook checks
